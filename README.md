# eurial-group — multi-brand, multi-location group website (demo)

This is the group site for **Eurial**. It follows the Neo Motors idea: a group gateway, with one site per brand.

It adds two things Neo Motors doesn't have:
- a layer for the **7 locations**;
- one search across the stock of all brands.

It carries the **Sales Partner logo** for the demo.

## Data

- **Real Eurial public data (October 2026):**
  - the 7 locations, their addresses and emails;
  - call centre 021 9160;
  - brands per location (from each `eurial{location}.ro` site);
  - departments per location (from eurial.ro/locatii).
  
  Otopeni is the only location with ITP. Otopeni and Brașov are the only ones with bodywork.
- **Indicative:** opening hours (Mon–Fri 08:00–18:00, Sat 09:00–14:00).
- **Omoda & Jaecoo:** shown at Pantelimon (eurialpantelimon.ro links to it).
- **Stock and "from" prices:** generated by `gen.js` (in the build folder) from the demo sites' `data.js`:
  - Peugeot `peugeot_dealership`;
  - Citroën `citroen_dealership`;
  - Omoda & Jaecoo `omoda-jaecoo`.
  
  The output goes to `assets/js/catalog.js`.
- **Images:**
  - Peugeot and Citroën: peugeot.ro and citroen.ro media libraries, as used in the brand demos.
  - Omoda & Jaecoo: omodajaecoo-iota.vercel.app.
  - DS and Opel: Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 4.0), credited on `legal.html?doc=credite`.

## Pages

| Page | Content |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | Hero, brand bar, stock search (brand / location / fuel / gearbox / price), brand tiles with location count, discounted stock, service band, 7 locations, services, news |
| `stoc.html` | New-car stock for all brands and locations, with filters and sorting |
| `modele.html` | The range of every brand with "from" prices |
| `oferte.html` | Stock deals per brand, service and leasing offers |
| `service.html` | Booking: brand → job → location → day and slot → car and contact. Only locations that can do the job are offered (bodywork: Otopeni and Brașov; ITP: Otopeni; Omoda & Jaecoo: Pantelimon). |
| `servicii.html` | Leasing, Eurial Broker, Rabla, ITP Otopeni, parts, manuals |
| `locatii.html` | Location list, filterable by brand, department and county |
| `locatie.html?id=` | Location detail: map, brands, departments, stock at that location, contact, hours, and a note on the 301 redirect from the old domain |
| `marca.html?b=ds\|opel` | Brand page |
| `contact.html` | Contact form (subject, brand, location), plus the call centre |
| `despre.html`, `cariere.html`, `reclamatii.html` | About, careers, feedback and complaints |
| `legal.html?doc=gdpr\|cookies\|termeni\|credite` | Legal pages |
| `404.html` | Not-found page |

- **Languages:** RO by default, EN through the toggle or `?lang=en`.
- **Lead inbox:** every form creates a lead with its brand and location. The **DEMO** panel (bottom left) shows the leads.

## Run

- Open `index.html` directly, or deploy it on Vercel with Root Directory = `eurial-group`.
- Images, the Sales Partner logo and the maps need an internet connection.
- The site sends `noindex`, and `robots.txt` blocks crawlers.

## Production (Sales Partner)

- Stock: `GET /api/stock` (all brands and locations).
- Catalogue: `GET /api/catalog`.
- Forms: `POST /api/leads`, routed by brand, location and department.
- Service: `POST /api/service-bookings`, with slots from the workshop calendar.
- Used cars: the same DMS stock feeds Eurial Certified.
- Customer portal: one account for all brands.
